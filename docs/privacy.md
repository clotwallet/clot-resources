# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CLOT Wallet ("CLOT Wallet", "we", "us", or "our") processes personal data when you use the CLOT Wallet application and the services that support it. Contact us at [info@clot.app](mailto:info@clot.app) with questions or privacy requests.

The data processed depends on the features you use. This policy is a notice about our processing practices; using the application does not by itself constitute consent to every type of processing described below. Where consent is required, it must be obtained separately.

## 1. Wallet data and public blockchains

CLOT Wallet includes self-custody wallet functionality. Recovery phrases and private keys used for this functionality are managed on your device. Do not send recovery phrases, private keys, passwords, or one-time login codes to support.

Providing wallet functionality requires processing public wallet addresses, selected networks and assets, balance and transaction queries, and signed transactions submitted for broadcast. Public addresses and transaction information can be personal data when they can be linked to an individual. A public blockchain is not anonymous merely because it uses addresses instead of names.

Transactions broadcast to a public blockchain may be visible worldwide, copied by other parties, and retained indefinitely. We cannot alter or delete records from independently operated public blockchains. Removing a wallet from the application does not erase its blockchain history.

## 2. Information processed

### Wallet and service requests

When you request balances, transaction history, NFT information, exchange quotes, or transaction execution, the relevant services process information needed for that request. This may include public wallet addresses, transaction hashes, assets, amounts, networks, destination addresses, quote details, and transaction status.

### Technical information

Network connections expose technical information such as an IP address to the servers receiving the connection. Our supporting infrastructure processes IP addresses for request handling and abuse prevention. Service logs can contain request methods and paths, response status, processing time, and error details. Some request paths contain public wallet addresses or other request identifiers.

Third-party services contacted directly by the application may also receive technical information about your connection. Information received by a provider through a CLOT proxy can differ from information received through a direct connection.

### Account, payment, and identity features

Where these features are available and you choose to use them, additional information is processed:

- Email sign-in: your email address, verification challenge and response, and authentication or session information.
- Purchases and sales: order information, amounts, assets, destination accounts or wallet addresses, and, where needed for a payout, the account holder's name, bank identifier, and bank account number.
- Identity verification: name, date of birth, document type and number, issuing country, expiry date, document photographs, and a selfie, when requested for the relevant service.

Payment and identity services may be subject to additional notices identifying the responsible operator, recipients, and applicable retention requirements. These notices must be available when the relevant information is requested. Wallet functionality and identity verification involve different data; the existence of a wallet address does not mean we have verified its owner's identity.

### Communications

If you contact us, we receive the contact details, message content, and attachments you provide. Include only information necessary for your request. If you use an external messaging service to contact us, that service also processes information under its own privacy policy.

## 3. Why information is processed

We process information to:

- Provide the features you request, including blockchain queries, transaction broadcasting, exchanges, and available account or payment services.
- Authenticate users and maintain sessions for features that require sign-in.
- Handle support and privacy requests and communicate about relevant service or security matters.
- Diagnose failures, maintain service reliability, prevent abuse, and protect the service and its users.
- Meet applicable legal obligations and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Where a law such as the GDPR applies, processing must have an applicable legal basis. Depending on the activity, this may be necessity for performing a contract with you or taking steps at your request, compliance with a legal obligation, legitimate interests in operating and securing the service subject to your rights, or consent where required. You may withdraw consent for consent-based processing without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

This policy does not give us blanket permission to send promotional communications or disclose information for unrelated marketing purposes.

## 4. Recipients and external services

Information needed to fulfil a request is disclosed to the infrastructure and service providers involved in that request. Relevant categories include hosting and network providers, blockchain nodes and explorers, NFT and market-data services, exchange providers, authentication and email services, and payment or identity-verification providers where those features are used.

The wallet's supporting integrations include NOWNodes, Helius, and Blockscout for blockchain access, OpenSea for NFT information, and SwapKit for exchange functionality. Available integrations vary by network, feature, and application version. A provider receives information relevant to the requests it handles; this does not mean every provider receives all information described in this policy.

If you connect to a third-party application through WalletConnect/Reown, connection services process session-related information, and the application you connect to receives the accounts and requests shared through that connection. Review connection and signing requests carefully. Disconnecting a session does not erase information already received or revoke permissions recorded on a blockchain.

External content, including token images, NFT media, and linked websites, may be retrieved from third-party servers. Those requests can disclose your IP address and the requested resource to the receiving server.

Providers processing data on our behalf and third parties determining their own purposes have different responsibilities. Independent services have their own privacy notices. A link to another service does not replace our responsibility for processing under our control.

We may also disclose relevant information where required by law, in response to a valid legal request, or where legally permitted and necessary to protect rights, investigate abuse, or resolve disputes. If a business reorganisation or transfer affects personal data, applicable safeguards and notice requirements continue to apply.

## 5. Retention

Retention depends on the information and its purpose:

- Device-held wallet information is managed through the application and your device. Copies or backups you create may remain separately.
- Account and session information is retained as needed to provide and secure the relevant account features and handle account closure.
- Technical records are retained for operational troubleshooting and security needs; records needed to investigate an incident may be retained for the investigation and related claims.
- Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve the request and deal with related follow-up or disputes.
- Payment and identity records may be subject to mandatory retention periods under the law applicable to the responsible operator, as explained in the relevant service notice.

Information should be deleted or anonymised when its purposes and applicable retention obligations no longer require it. Contact us for information about the retention applicable to your data. These criteria do not apply to the independent retention of public blockchain records by third parties.

## 6. International processing

The services you use may involve providers or blockchain participants in countries other than your country of residence. Data-protection rules may differ between countries. Where restrictions on international transfers apply to processing under our control, an appropriate lawful transfer mechanism is required, such as an applicable adequacy decision or contractual safeguards. Contact us for information about the destinations and safeguards relevant to your data.

Acceptance of this policy is not a substitute for a legally required transfer mechanism. Broadcasting information to a public blockchain also makes it available to participants globally, as described above.

## 7. Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access your personal data, obtain a copy, correct inaccurate information, request deletion, restrict processing, object to processing, and obtain data portability. You may also have the right to withdraw consent and complain to a competent data-protection authority.

Send requests to [info@clot.app](mailto:info@clot.app), explaining the feature involved and the action you request. We may need proportionate information to verify that the request concerns you. We will not ask for a recovery phrase or private key to verify a privacy request. We will respond within the time required by applicable law and explain any applicable limitation or refusal.

Deleting an application, disconnecting a third-party service, or removing a local wallet does not automatically delete information already held by service providers. Deletion requests concern information under our control and remain subject to applicable legal retention obligations. We cannot erase public blockchain records or information independently controlled by another service.

You can decline to use optional features or provide information they require, although this may prevent those features from working. Review device permissions and third-party connection settings to manage the access you grant.

## 8. Security

We use safeguards intended to protect information processed by the service. No internet transmission, device, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device and wallet recovery information secure, and report suspected security issues through our contact address without including secret credentials.

## 9. Children

The service is intended for adults aged 18 or older, consistent with our Terms and Conditions. We do not knowingly collect children's personal data for use of the service. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where applicable.
